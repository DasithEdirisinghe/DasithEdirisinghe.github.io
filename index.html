About

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Vision at the SAIVT Research Centre, Queensland University of Technology, Brisbane, Australia.

My research focuses on robust AI for autonomous perception, particularly 3D perception and representation learning. I am also interested in efficient deep learning, model optimization, and accelerating AI training and inference on modern hardware.

I hold a First Class Honours B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.
